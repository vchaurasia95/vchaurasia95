I specialize in the intersection of **Data Engineering** and **Agentic AI**. My work focuses on architecting self-healing data pipelines and leveraging multi-agent systems (using Google ADK) to automate complex enterprise workflows.

Currently, I am defining how **Large Language Models (LLMs)** can act as orchestrators for data quality and PII detection in high-volume environments.
